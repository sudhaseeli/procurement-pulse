Procurement Pulse

I went through seven years of Department of Veterans Affairs contracts to see who VA buys from, how much of it is really competed, and where it depends on a single supplier.

    Which supplies depend on one company?

    HHI is the Herfindahl–Hirschman index: the sum of each supplier's squared percentage share. Antitrust agencies treat a market above 1,800 as highly concentrated, and 10,000 means a single supplier.

    How much buying is competed?

    Sole-source means nobody else was asked. Single bid means the contract was opened to competition but only one company showed up. Each new award counts once, in the year it was signed.

    The September rush

    Most yearly funding expires if it isn't spent by 30 September, so agencies spend fast at the end of the year. I wanted to know whether the rushed money still gets competed.

    How I built it

    DownloadOne archive per year from USAspending.gov. Downloads resume if they drop, and every zip is checked before use.
    CleanDuckDB reads the raw files, sorts actions into fiscal years and groups subsidiaries under their parent.
    AnalyseSpend, supplier concentration, competition, contract growth and year-end tables, all in SQL.
    CheckThe dollars have to match from the raw rows to every number on this page, or nothing gets published.
    PublishOne JSON file powers this page on GitHub Pages.
